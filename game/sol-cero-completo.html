SOL ZERO · PROTOCOL ARES-7 (BASED ON NASA STUDIES)

01 — FATIGUE & ISOLATION (NASA HRP)

Sleep or finish a task. NASA links sleep deprivation and circadian desynchronization with cognitive and motor decline. Continuing work during rest hours accumulates fatigue (slowing repairs and requiring extra verification checks), while sleeping restores performance at the cost of leaving unfixed systems offline until the next Sol.

02 — DUST STORMS & ELECTRICAL POWER BUDGET

The real danger is darkness. Suspended atmospheric dust and dust deposited on solar panels slash power generation. Cleaning the panels at `x 800` removes deposited dust, but during a storm you must shed non-essential loads (`[B]`) so life support batteries do not drain.

03 — OXYGEN AVAILABLE, HAZARDOUS AIR (NASA ECLSS)

Beyond O₂. The ECLSS system regulates oxygen, pressure (`kPa`), and carbon dioxide (`CO₂`). A failure in `CO₂` scrubbing makes a module lethal even when `O₂` tanks are full. You can isolate the module (`[V]`) or decide whether to spend your single spare filter to save the greenhouse or reserve it for the main habitat.
